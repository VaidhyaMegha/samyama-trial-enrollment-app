# Regulatory Terminology Guide
## Clinical Trial Management System

### Purpose
This document defines regulatory-compliant terminology used throughout the Trial Compass Pro platform to ensure compliance with ICH-GCP guidelines and prevent protocol deviations.

---

## Key Terminology

### Screening
**Definition:** The process of evaluating whether a patient meets all inclusion and exclusion criteria for a clinical trial.

**Key Points:**
- Occurs BEFORE enrollment
- Includes comprehensive patient assessment
- Requires verification of all eligibility criteria
- Must be documented in source documents
- May include screening visit procedures

**Platform Usage:**
- "Approved for Screening" button
- "Screening Progress" metrics
- "Screening Visit" workflow step

---

### Enrollment
**Definition:** The formal process of registering a patient into a clinical trial AFTER successful screening and eligibility confirmation.

**Key Points:**
- Occurs AFTER screening visit
- Requires confirmed eligibility
- Involves informed consent process
- Marks official study participation start
- Must be documented per protocol

**Important:** The platform uses "screening" terminology for the pre-enrollment phase. "Enrollment" refers to the formal registration after screening completion.

---

### AI Match / Preliminary Match
**Definition:** A preliminary assessment generated by AI analysis to identify potential patient-protocol matches.

**Key Points:**
- **NOT a confirmation of eligibility**
- Requires clinical validation
- Must be verified during screening visit
- Used for efficiency, not decision-making
- Subject to two-level approval (CRC + PI)

**Platform Disclaimers:**
> "Approval is based on preliminary AI match. Eligibility must be confirmed at the screening visit."

---

### Screening Visit
**Definition:** A mandatory in-person comprehensive assessment where all eligibility criteria are verified before enrollment.

**Key Points:**
- Required by ICH-GCP guidelines
- All inclusion/exclusion criteria rechecked
- Physical examination performed
- Laboratory tests obtained and reviewed
- Medical history verified
- Informed consent obtained (if not previously done)

---

## Clinical Trial Workflow

```
1. AI Pre-Screening
   ↓
2. CRC Review & Approval
   ↓
3. PI Review & Approval
   ↓
4. SCREENING VISIT (Mandatory)
   ↓
5. Eligibility Confirmation
   ↓
6. Informed Consent
   ↓
7. ENROLLMENT (Official Study Start)
```

## Regulatory Compliance Guidelines

### ICH-GCP Requirements
1. **Protocol Adherence**: All eligibility criteria must be verified per protocol
2. **Documentation**: Source documents must support eligibility determinations
3. **PI Responsibility**: Principal Investigator maintains final responsibility for eligibility
4. **Informed Consent**: Must be obtained before study-specific procedures
5. **Protocol Deviations**: Failure to confirm eligibility at screening = protocol deviation

### Platform Safeguards
- Regulatory disclaimers on all approval workflows
- "Screening" terminology instead of "Enrollment" for pre-enrollment phase
- Mandatory two-level approval (CRC + PI)
- Reminder messages about screening visit requirement
- Audit trail documentation

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Incorrect Usage
- "Patient is enrolled" (after AI match approval)
- "Proceed with enrollment" (without screening visit)
- "Enrollment approved" (for preliminary match)
- Using AI match as final eligibility determination

### ✅ Correct Usage
- "Patient is approved for screening"
- "Proceed with screening visit"
- "Screening approval granted"
- "AI match requires screening visit confirmation"
